## Cross-Project Locks

- No site imports another site renderer, CSS, header, hero, card, or section helper.
- Each route uses only its declared project-owned bitmap asset keys.
- No photo, crop, person, room, machine, food arrangement, or composition is reused across projects.
- Browser frame and URL are shared; the rendered site anatomy is not.
- All final screenshots remain 1920x1280 desktop and contain meaningful detail in the lower quarter.
